About

Who We Are

3note advisors is a single family office dedicated to long-term capital appreciation. We manage a diversified portfolio spanning public equities, digital assets, private credit, venture capital, and private equity — always with an emphasis on rigorous analysis and patient capital deployment.


Our approach blends institutional discipline with the flexibility and long-term horizon that a family office affords. We are not constrained by quarterly benchmarks or short-term thinking — we invest with conviction where we see enduring value.

How We Invest

Public Markets

Private Markets

Selective allocations to venture capital, private equity, and private credit, seeking asymmetric returns with strong downside protection.

Long-Term Horizon

Our permanent capital structure allows us to underwrite opportunities that require patience, avoiding the constraints of traditional fund cycles.
